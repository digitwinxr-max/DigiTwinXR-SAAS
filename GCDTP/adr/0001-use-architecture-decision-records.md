# ADR-0001: Use Architecture Decision Records

## Status

Accepted

## Context

As the GCDTP project grows, it will be important to track architectural decisions made during development. Without a systematic approach, the reasoning behind decisions may be lost over time, leading to confusion and potential rework.

## Decision

We will use Architecture Decision Records (ADRs) to document significant architectural decisions. Each ADR will capture:
- The decision made
- The context that led to the decision
- The consequences of the decision

## Consequences

### Positive
- Clear traceability of architectural choices
- Improved onboarding for new team members
- Reduced knowledge loss over time
- Better alignment on technical direction

### Negative
- Additional overhead for documentation
- Requires discipline to maintain

### Neutral
- Decisions may become outdated and need review
